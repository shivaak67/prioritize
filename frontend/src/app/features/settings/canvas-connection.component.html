About your connection and privacy

Your private feed link is encrypted on the server and hidden after connection. Supports Canvas sites at *.instructure.com.

Imported items are read-only; update them in Canvas. The feed does not include grades, submissions, or undated to-do items. Canvas usually includes the last 30 days and next 366 days; items no longer in the feed are removed from this calendar.

After connecting, open Calendar or Tasks → Canvas assignments to see your deadlines. Schedule alerts for several assignments together in Reminders.
